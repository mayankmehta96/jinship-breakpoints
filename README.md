# jinship-breakpoints

Breakpoints is a story-intake and signal-mapping platform for the social safety net. People share what happened to them in their own words, then add lightweight structure such as what program they needed help with, what difficulty they faced, when it happened, and where.

The goal is to turn lived experiences into public evidence about where support systems are breaking. Instead of treating each story as an isolated anecdote, Breakpoints groups repeated experiences into patterns that show where people are getting lost, delayed, denied, or dropped from help.

What data Breakpoints is trying to compile:


1. Barrier volume
Counts of the most common barriers by:
– Program
– Geography
– Time period
– Population segment, if appropriate and privacy-safe

2. Friction maps
A map of exactly where in a process people are getting stuck, such as:
– Finding the right program
– Starting an application
– Gathering documents
– Scheduling an appointment
– Attending an interview
– Receiving a decision
– Completing renewal or recertification
- Using or retaining benefits

3. Place-based patterns
Breakpoints is also aiming to see show which ZIP codes, neighborhoods, counties, or regions are reporting concentrated problems and what those problems are.

4. Emerging signals
Because stories can be collected before formal application data appears, Breakpoints can act as an early warning system for rising need or new administrative burden.
