# jinship-breakpoints

Breakpoints is a story-intake and signal-mapping platform for the social safety net. People share what happened to them in their own words, then add lightweight structure such as what program they needed help with, what difficulty they faced, when it happened, and where.

The goal is to turn lived experiences into public evidence about where support systems are breaking. Instead of treating each story as an isolated anecdote, Breakpoints groups repeated experiences into patterns that show where people are getting lost, delayed, denied, or dropped from help.
